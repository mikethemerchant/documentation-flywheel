# Interview Brief Template

Copy into `evidence/interviews/<person-slug>.md`. One file per person, refreshed
each round from [question-register.md](../evidence/question-register.md).

**Only prep briefs for conversations that are actually coming up.** A brief for
a meeting with no date is a to-do list wearing a costume, and it is the start of
the pile of documents nobody reads.

Delete the guidance in italics as you fill it in.

## Why <Name>

*One or two sentences. What they uniquely hold that nobody else does. If you
cannot write this, the brief is being prepped out of habit.*

## How they think

*Two or three lines from [context/people.md](../../context/people.md), plus the
practical consequence: how to phrase things so they land.*

*This is the section that makes the difference between a list of questions and
a brief. Knowing that one person answers in recovery windows and another in
transaction paths tells you how to ask, and asking the right way is most of
getting a real answer.*
